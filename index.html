What do AOS / Max Elevation / LOS mean?
AOS (Acquisition of Signal) = the satellite rises above your chosen minimum elevation. Max Elevation = the highest point reached during that pass. LOS (Loss of Signal) = the satellite drops below the minimum elevation. These are calculated predictions from orbital data, not live radio-contact confirmations.
